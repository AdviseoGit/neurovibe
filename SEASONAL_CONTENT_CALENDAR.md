## CONTENT PRODUCTION WORKFLOW

### 4 Weeks Before Peak Traffic
1. **Research:** GSC queries, trend tools, Reddit/forums
2. **Outline:** Structure, keywords, schema.org plan
3. **Draft:** Write 2000-3000 words, add interactive elements

### 2 Weeks Before Peak Traffic
4. **Optimize:** Add schema, internal links, lead magnets
5. **Review:** Check mobile, test forms, verify links
6. **Publish:** Deploy, submit to GSC, share internally

### During Peak Traffic
7. **Monitor:** Track GSC daily, check lead conversion
8. **Adjust:** Update title/meta if CTR low, add FAQs if needed

### After Peak Traffic
9. **Analyze:** Document what worked (clicks, leads, rankings)
10. **Update Calendar:** Refine next year's plan based on data
