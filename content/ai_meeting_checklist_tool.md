# Blueprint: Neuroinclusive Meeting Checklist Generator

## Concept
A web-based interactive tool that generates customized meeting guidelines and checklists based on the meeting type, duration, and participant neurotypes (if known, or general best practices).

## Value Proposition
Many neurodivergent individuals find traditional meetings draining (sensory overload, lack of clear agendas, unstructured social time). This tool helps managers and meeting organizers instantly create meeting structures that reduce cognitive load and improve outcomes for everyone.

## Features
- **Meeting Type Selector:** (e.g., Brainstorming, Status Update, 1-on-1, All Hands)
- **Duration Selector:** (e.g., 15m, 30m, 1h+)
- **Format:** (Remote, Hybrid, In-person)
- **Output:** A copy-pasteable meeting invite template and a facilitator checklist (e.g., "Send agenda 24h prior", "Allow camera-off for remote", "Provide written summary").
