
# Neurovibe Weekly Report: 2026-06-03

## 1. SEO Analysis

This week's SEO analysis focused on identifying content gaps and opportunities in the Swedish market related to neurodiversity in the workplace.

### Key Findings:

*   **"ADHD anpassningar jobb":** This is a highly competitive keyword, with the top search results dominated by established health and career websites. Neurovibe's existing content for this keyword was not ranking in the top 10.
*   **"Maskering på arbetsplatsen":** A significant content gap was identified for this keyword. The search results were sparse and not directly relevant to the concept of masking in the context of neurodiversity. This presents a high-priority opportunity for Neurovibe to become an authority on the topic.
*   **"Neurodiversitet på jobbet":** The search results for this term were a mix of Swedish and Danish content, indicating that the topic is not yet saturated in the Swedish market. This presents another opportunity for Neurovibe to create foundational content.

## 2. Content Creation and Improvements

Based on the SEO analysis, the following actions were taken:

### New Content:

*   **Article: "Vad är Maskering? En Guide för Neurodivergenta på Arbetsplatsen"**
    *   **File:** `projects/neurovibe/content/maskering-en-guide.md`
    *   **Purpose:** To fill the identified content gap and provide a comprehensive resource on the topic of masking for a Swedish audience. The article defines masking, explains its impact, and offers strategies for unmasking.

### Content Improvements:

*   **Article: "Från Kaos till Kontroll: Din Guide till ADHD-Anpassningar på Jobbet"**
    *   **File:** `projects/neurovibe/static/adhd-anpassningar-jobb-v2.html`
    *   **Improvements:**
        *   **New Title and Meta Description:** The title and meta description were updated to be more engaging and keyword-rich.
        *   **Expanded Content:** A new section on "Kommunikation och Självledarskap" was added to provide more comprehensive advice.
        *   **Internal Linking:** An internal link to the new "maskering" article was added to improve SEO and user experience.
        *   **Keyword Optimization:** The content was updated to include a broader range of relevant keywords, such as "stöd", "strategier", and "hjälpmedel".
